# Gallery

Every persona in every state, recorded on the desktop build
([docs/testing.md](testing.md)). Each persona has:

- **Emotions**: every mood and reaction, with a caption.
- **Activities**: the voice assistant, attention, offline, waking, a focus
  block's states, the menu and the Weather app in the persona's theme, a
  message and a doorbell card.
- **Tour**, **apps** and **prompts**: a day with a focus block and the
  daily goal, Tasks and Calendar, a doorbell and a quiz.

The GIFs are large. They load as you scroll.
